About us

We created Brass Leaf to give North Scottsdale an upscale cigar lounge with the kind of hospitality that makes you want to become a regular.

At Hayden Peak Crossings on North Hayden Road, we're building a place where you can settle into a favorite seat, enjoy a fine cigar, and spend the evening in good company.

The cigar sets the tone. We can help you find one in our walk-in humidor, pour a bourbon or a glass of wine that brings out its character, and serve you a meal worth making an evening of. Live jazz gives the room its rhythm, and our team pays attention to the little things, from remembering what you like to knowing when to give you time to enjoy it.

That's what makes Brass Leaf special to us. Every part of the evening belongs together, and every guest should feel like they do, too.

Membership

The wine club has two tiers. Every other month, one tier or the other has a wine social to pour the wines our sommelier has selected.

Both tiers include two curated bottles every other month, tastings, and access to the members' lounge. The Enthusiast tier includes lounge access for one guest, and Connoisseur includes access for up to three guests.

The proposed Cigar VIP membership program is coming soon, with the top tier VIP membership including a joint cigar & wine membership.

We are also developing a new membership program that will bring together both our cigar and wine offerings. The program is intended to provide members with additional benefits, exclusive experiences, and access to select amenities within Brass Leaf. We are currently working through the membership structure, benefits, and pricing, and will share those details once finalized. Our goal is to create a program that appeals to both cigar enthusiasts and wine connoisseurs, while offering a unique experience for those who appreciate both.

Membership itself will live on our main website. Until then, leave your email and tell us which program you're interested in.

Good to know

The questions we hear most, answered plainly.

Are you open now?

Yes. We are open now as a restaurant with excellent food, music and smoking on the patio. Hours are Tuesday to Saturday from 3 pm to 9 pm and Sunday from 3 pm to 8 pm. We are closed on Monday.

Where can I smoke a cigar?

On the patio. Cigars are smoked on the patio only.

When does the walk-in humidor open?

Soon. An exact date is currently not available. Leave your email on this page and we'll write to you when it opens.

I don't smoke. Is it for me?

Yes, you're welcome here. Cigars are smoked on the patio only.

Do I need a reservation?

Not required but recommended.

How long does a cigar take?

Most guests give one an hour and a half or two, which leaves time for a drink and something to eat.

Do you serve food?

Yes, a full menu from a classically trained chef.

Is there a membership?

Yes. Membership will change from the old Pinard wine membership. Current members will be grandfathered.
